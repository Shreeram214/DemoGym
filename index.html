About IronPulse

More than a gym. A community.

Founded in Pune in 2017, IronPulse Fitness was built for people who take their training seriously — and for those just getting started. Across 12,000 sq. ft. we combine world-class equipment, expert coaching and a supportive community to help you reach your health and wellness goals.

  • Modern Equipment

    Hammer Strength, Rogue racks and the latest cardio machines.

  • Certified Trainers

    ACE, NASM and ACSM-certified coaches on the floor daily.

  • Personalized Training

    Programs tailored to your body, schedule and goals.

  • Flexible Membership

    Monthly or annual plans — upgrade or pause anytime.
